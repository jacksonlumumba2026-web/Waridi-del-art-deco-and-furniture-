About Us

Waridi DelArt Decor & Furniture

We are a Nairobi-based furniture and interior finishing studio. We build, reupholster and restore furniture, handle joinery and woodwork, and dress interiors with curtains, blinds, carpets and wall finishes — for homes, offices, hotels, guest rooms and Airbnb properties, delivered and fitted countrywide.

Nairobi-based, serving clients countrywide — we travel to you for delivery and installation, wherever you are in Kenya.
