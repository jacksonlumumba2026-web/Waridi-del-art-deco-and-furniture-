About Us

Waridi DelArt Decor and Furniture

We are a Nairobi-based furniture and interior finishing company. We build, reupholster and restore furniture, handle joinery and woodwork, and dress interiors with curtains, blinds, carpets and wall finishes — for homes, offices, hotels, guest rooms and Airbnb properties.

Vision

To be Nairobi's most trusted name in bespoke furniture, upholstery and interior finishing — transforming every space we touch.

Mission

To create, update and renovate furniture and interiors with honest craftsmanship, quality materials and close attention to detail — on time, every time.
